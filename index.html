What a hire includes

  • Delivery, set-up and anchoring — the two of us, not a courier
  • A safety briefing before anyone climbs on
  • The blower and a 30 m extension lead
  • Collection the same day, once the party is over

Delivery

Free for the first 15 km. From 15 to 30 km it is R8 a kilometre for the distance past 15 km. Beyond 30 km it is R12 a kilometre for that furthest stretch.

So a 25 km run is R80, and a 40 km run is R240. We tell you the exact figure when we quote — there is never a number you did not see first.

Good to know

What surface do you need? Grass or sand, so we can peg every anchor point. Paving and tar are possible with ballast, but tell us when you book so we bring it.
Do you need electricity? Yes. A normal household plug within about thirty metres. We bring the extension lead.
Who supervises the children? An adult over eighteen, the whole time. The castle is never left running unattended, and that responsibility sits with you in writing.
How far ahead should we book? Two weeks in peak season, October to January. Off-season we can often manage a few days’ notice.

Safety

  • R5 million public liability cover, written for inflatables. Most event policies exclude them entirely
  • Every anchor point pegged, every time. We come down in high wind, and that is written into your agreement so it is never an argument on the day
  • A signed hire agreement on every booking, so everyone knows who is responsible for what before anyone climbs on

Where the name comes from. Ca from Caron, Re from Refilwe — the two of us. We deliver and collect every booking ourselves, so you meet the same faces each time.
