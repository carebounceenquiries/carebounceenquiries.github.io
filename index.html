    Questions

    The ones we always get

    What if we need to cancel?

    Cancel more than 24 hours before your party and you get a full refund, no questions asked. Inside 24 hours we hold the deposit as a credit towards another date, valid for 12 months. We do not simply keep it. Anything you paid above the deposit comes back either way.

    What if it rains?

    We watch it with you. If the weather makes it unsafe to set up, we move your booking to another date rather than refund it, and if no date that suits you can be agreed within 60 days, you get the deposit back in full. Do not cancel because of the forecast: phone us, because the call on the day is ours, and a cancelled booking inside 24 hours is only a credit.

    What does delivery cost?

    Free delivery for the first 15km from our Centurion base. Between 15km and 30km it is R8 a kilometre for the distance past 15km, and beyond 30km it is R12 a kilometre for that furthest stretch. So a 25km run is R80 and a 40km run is R240. We tell you the figure when we quote.

    How long do we have it for?

    The castles and soft play are yours for the day. We set up in the morning and collect once the party is over. The bubble house is a four-hour hire.

    How much space do we need?

    The two standard castles are 4×4 m and need about 6 × 6 m of clear flat ground, a metre free on every side. The castle with the slide is 8×4 m and needs about 10 × 6 m. We also have to carry it in from where we park, so we need a clear path at least one metre wide the whole way, gates and side passages included. If you are not sure it fits, send us a photo of the spot and we will tell you before you pay.

    What surface do you need?

    Grass or sand is ideal. Paving is possible with ballast, but tell us when you book so we bring it.

    Do you need electricity?

    Yes. A normal household plug within about thirty metres. We bring the extension lead.

    Who supervises the children?

    An adult over eighteen, the whole time. The castle is never left running unattended, and that responsibility sits with you in writing.

      Terms of hire

      The agreement, in plain words

      You sign this before we set up. It is short on purpose. You should be able to read all of it in two minutes and know exactly where you stand.

      This agreement is with CaRe Bounce Hire (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2026/855134/07, a company registered in South Africa and trading as CaRe Bounce. “We” and “us” below mean that company.

      1. Booking and payment

      A 50% deposit secures your date. The balance is due on the day, when we arrive and before we set up. Dates are held only once the deposit reflects. An EFT can take a day or two, so send us the proof of payment and we hold the date from that moment, rather than waiting for it to clear.

      2. What the price includes

      Setup, anchoring, a safety briefing and collection. We deliver and set up in the morning, and collect the same day once your party has finished, at a time we agree when you book.

      Free delivery for the first 15km. Between 15km and 30km we charge R8 per kilometre for the distance beyond 15km, and beyond 30km, R12 per kilometre for that further stretch. Distance is measured one way by road from our base in Centurion. We confirm the amount in writing when we quote, and it does not change afterwards.

      3. Where we can set up

      1. Grass, sand, or paving is possible, but please let us know when booking.
      2. A standard household plug within roughly thirty metres. We bring the extension lead.
      3. Clear flat ground with at least one metre free on every side of the unit. A 4×4 m castle therefore needs about 6 × 6 m; the 8×4 m castle with the slide needs about 10 × 6 m.
      4. A clear path from where we can park to where the castle goes, at least one metre wide the whole way, including any gates, side passages and doorways. Rolled up, a castle is about 1.3 m long and weighs up to 100 kg, so it cannot be lifted over a wall or squeezed through a narrow gap. Please measure the narrowest point before you book. If it will not fit on the day we cannot set up, and the hire is still payable.
      5. Dogs secured, and the area free of pet mess, sharp objects and overhanging branches.

      4. Supervision

      An adult over eighteen must supervise at all times while a unit is in use. The unit is never left running unattended. This responsibility is yours from the moment we finish the briefing until we collect.

      5. Rules while in use

      1. No shoes, spectacles, jewellery or sharp objects on the unit.
      2. No food, drink, chewing gum, face paint, silly string or party poppers on or near it.
      3. No somersaults or rough play. Children of a similar age on together.
      4. Everyone off if the blower stops, and immediately in high wind or lightning.

      6. Cancelling

      Cancel more than 24 hours before the delivery time and your deposit is refunded in full, no questions asked. Cancel within 24 hours and the deposit is held as a credit towards another date, valid for 12 months. Anything you have paid above the deposit is refunded.

      If the weather makes it unsafe to set up, we reschedule rather than refund, and we agree a new date with you. If no date that suits you can be agreed within 60 days, the deposit is refunded in full. Please do not cancel because of the forecast. Phone us, because the call on the day is ours to make.

      If we call it off for any other reason, such as equipment failure or anything else on our side, you are refunded in full, whenever that happens, including on the day. Refunds go back to the account the deposit came from, within seven business days.

      7. Damage, cleaning and loss

      Fair wear is on us. Grass marks, dust and ordinary party mess cost you nothing.

      Damage from misuse, or from breaking the rules in clause 5, is charged at repair cost.

      If a unit comes back heavily soiled, a cleaning fee of R600 applies. Heavily soiled means something that has to be washed off and dried before the unit can be hired again, such as vomit, pet mess, face paint, silly string, or food and mud trodden into the seams. The fee reflects what it costs us: a soiled unit has to be washed and then dried right through before it can be rolled, which takes it out of service for the next day.

      We photograph each unit when we collect it, and will send you those photographs with any charge.

      If a unit is stolen or destroyed while in your care, replacement cost applies.

      8. Insurance and liability

      We are fully insured for public liability. That cover responds to our negligence, our equipment, our setup, our anchoring. It does not cover injury arising from unsupervised use or from breaking these terms, which remains your responsibility as the hirer.

      9. Your information

      We keep your name, contact number and address to deliver your booking and to contact you about it. We do not sell or share it. You may ask us to delete it once your booking is complete.
